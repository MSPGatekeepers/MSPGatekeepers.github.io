Privacy Policy

Gate Keeper SMS Reminder System
MSP Gatekeepers — Last updated: May 2026

Overview

This SMS reminder system is operated by MSP Gatekeepers, a private church organization. It sends automated text message reminders to volunteer members who are scheduled to serve as gate keepers at church meetings.

Information We Collect

We collect only the first name and mobile phone number of volunteer members who have agreed to receive SMS reminders. No other personal information is collected or stored.

How We Use Your Information

Phone numbers are used solely to send scheduled SMS reminders about upcoming gate keeper duties. We do not use your information for marketing, advertising, or any other purpose.

Text Message Frequency

Members will receive no more than 4 text messages per week, only on days they are scheduled for duty.

Opting Out

You may opt out of receiving SMS reminders at any time by replying STOP to any message. You will receive no further messages after opting out.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose your personal information to any third party, except to Twilio, our SMS delivery provider, solely for the purpose of sending text messages on our behalf.

Data Security

Phone numbers are stored securely and are accessible only to the system administrator.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or to be removed from the reminder list, please contact your church administrator directly.
